Shipping Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026.

No physical fulfilment

Curated Wear Avenue does not currently sell or dispatch physical goods. There are no shipping fees, delivery estimates, tracking numbers or destination restrictions associated with using this editorial website.

Products discussed in articles

Our guides may discuss general garment categories and shopping considerations. They do not confirm that a particular item is in stock or deliverable. Shipping options, duties, taxes, customs, delivery times and geographic restrictions are determined by the third-party seller and carrier.

External order support

For an order placed elsewhere, consult the seller's confirmation and current shipping policy. Contact that seller or its carrier for tracking, delay, loss or damage enquiries. Curated Wear Avenue cannot view or change an external order.

Future changes

If physical products are offered in future, complete dispatch locations, processing estimates, shipping methods, charges, customs information and support procedures will be displayed before checkout and reflected here.
