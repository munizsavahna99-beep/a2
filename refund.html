Refund Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026.

No purchases on this website

Curated Wear Avenue is an editorial publication. We do not sell clothing, subscriptions, digital downloads or services, and we do not collect payments. Therefore there is currently no purchase from this website to cancel or refund.

Third-party purchases

If you followed an external link and purchased from another business, that seller is responsible for fulfilment, cancellation, returns and refunds under its displayed terms and applicable law. Contact the seller directly using the order confirmation. We cannot access third-party orders or approve refunds on their behalf.

Unexpected charge

A bank statement showing “Curated Wear Avenue” should not result from this website. First confirm the merchant descriptor with your bank or payment provider. If you believe our name has been misused, send non-sensitive details through our Contact page. Never email full card numbers, passwords or security codes.

Future changes

If we later introduce a paid product, a clear price, seller identity, fulfilment information and applicable refund terms will be shown before payment. This page will be updated before any such feature launches.
