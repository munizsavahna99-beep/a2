Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026. This policy explains how Curated Wear Avenue handles information when you visit this independent editorial website.

Information we receive

We do not operate user accounts, process orders or collect payment details. If you contact us, we receive the name, email address and message you choose to provide. Basic technical information such as browser type, approximate region, pages viewed and device category may be processed through Google Analytics only after consent.

Cookies and analytics

Analytics storage is denied by default. If you select “Allow analytics,” Google Analytics 4 may use cookies or similar identifiers to help us understand aggregate use. You may decline without losing access to editorial content. You can clear browser storage to reset your choice.

Purpose and legal basis

Contact information is used to answer enquiries, correct articles and address privacy or accessibility requests. Consent is the basis for optional analytics. We use reasonable security measures and retain correspondence only as long as needed for the enquiry, legal obligations or legitimate record keeping.

Sharing and international processing

We do not sell personal information. Service providers may process limited data to provide hosting, email or analytics. Those providers apply their own safeguards and terms, and processing may occur outside your country.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of contact information, subject to applicable law. Send a clear request through the Contact page. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

This general-interest website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
